The problem

Context

Urban delivery in Nairobi is constrained by traffic congestion, rising fuel costs, and unreliable last-mile logistics. Businesses, NGOs, and individuals depend on motorbike deliveries, yet the current ecosystem is fragmented and inefficient.

Petrol-powered bikes dominate the market, creating high operating costs and environmental impact while limiting rider earnings.

The E-Fikishe approach

Solution

Electric-first

We focus on electric motorbikes to reduce fuel dependency, operating costs, and emissions.

Platform model

E-Fikishe aggregates independent riders into a single dispatch and booking system.

Local scale

Designed specifically for Kenyan cities, with expansion potential across East Africa.

Why Nairobi

Market

Nairobi has one of the highest motorbike delivery densities in the region. Electric bikes are becoming more affordable and accessible, creating a unique opportunity for clean, efficient logistics.

  • Growing e-commerce and SME demand
  • Increasing adoption of electric bikes
  • Severe traffic congestion favoring two-wheel mobility

Current status

Pilot phase
  • Platform: Private preview
  • Bookings: Early access testing
  • Rider network: Onboarding pilot
  • Public launch: Planned

Team & vision

Founding

E-Fikishe is built by a small, focused team of technologists and operators with experience in urban mobility and digital platforms.

Our vision is to make electric last-mile delivery affordable, reliable, and scalable across African cities.
