About E‑Fikishe

E‑Fikishe is an intent-driven demo of a modern, low-friction electric delivery platform. This About page focuses on clear, readable content and accessible structure for global audiences.

Mission

Reduce urban delivery cost and emissions by providing a marketplace for electric last-mile transport, with a focus on operational simplicity and rider welfare.

Approach

  • Electric-first fleet and partner integrations.
  • Local operations with global design standards.
  • Accessible product and clear reporting.
