E-fikishe — Business Proposal

1. Executive Summary

Company Name: E-fikishe

Location: Kenya (Starting in Nairobi, with plans to expand nationwide)

Industry: E-mobility, Consultancy, Last-Mile Delivery, Renewable Energy, Solar Grid Installation, Carbon Removal.

Mission: To provide convenient, affordable, and eco-friendly last-mile delivery solutions using electric bikes (e-bikes) and electric tuk-tuks (e-tuktuks) while ensuring sustainability.

Vision: To become Africa’s leading e-mobility platform, revolutionizing last-mile logistics while creating job opportunities and reducing carbon emissions.

---

2. Problem Statement

E-fikishe plans to address carbon, inefficient late deliveries and unemployment challenges by introducing a clean-energy, tech-driven solution that benefits businesses, individuals, and the environment.

- High Costs: Rising fuel prices and maintenance costs for traditional delivery motorcycles and tuk-tuks.
- Inefficiency: Unreliable and slow delivery services in urban areas.
- Environmental Impact: High carbon emissions from fossil fuel-powered vehicles.
- Lack of Charging Infrastructure: Limited access to reliable charging stations for electric vehicles (EVs).

---

3. Solution

E-fikishe will provide:

- E-Bikes and E-Tuktuks
- AI-Powered App
- Solar Charging Stations
- Personalized EV Installation Services
- Cold Chains for perishables

Action Points: Direct sales & leasing, B2B partnerships, Charging infrastructure, Subscription & pay-per-use models, Carbon credit monetization.

---

4. Market Analysis

Target Market: B2B clients, B2C customers, E-Riders.

Market Opportunity: Kenya’s e-commerce sector and Nairobi's congestion provide demand for last-mile EVs.

Competitive Advantage: Sustainability, AI-driven tech, convenience, job creation, cost savings.

---

5. Business Model

Revenue Streams: Delivery fees, leasing/rental, charging services, EV installs, carbon credits.

Cost Structure: Vehicles, infrastructure, operations, partnerships.

---

6. Operations Plan

Key Activities: Procure vehicles, setup solar charging stations, app development, rider training, logistics partnerships.

---

7. Technology and Innovation

AI-Powered App: Geomapping, dynamic pricing, real-time tracking, rider management.

Solar Charging Stations: Strategically placed microgrids with pay-per-use.

---

8. Marketing Strategy

Branding, social campaigns, partnerships, community engagement, discounts for first-time clients.

---

9. Financial Plan

Overview: Procurement, charging infrastructure, tech development, marketing, operational scale-up.

Funding strategies: Investors, grants, partnerships.

---

10. Scalability

City expansion, additional services, franchising opportunity.

---

11. Impact and Sustainability

Environmental Impact: Reduced emissions.
Social Impact: Job creation.
Economic Impact: Lower delivery costs.

---

12. Next Steps

1. Pilot in Nairobi
2. Secure funding
3. Deploy charging stations and launch the app
4. Scale operations
