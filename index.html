The Problem We're Solving

Urban delivery in Nairobi faces critical challenges that impact businesses, riders, and the environment.

High Delivery Costs

Fuel and maintenance costs consume 40-60% of delivery revenue, making last-mile logistics unaffordable for small businesses.

Carbon Emissions

Traditional motorbikes contribute significantly to urban air pollution and carbon footprint in rapidly growing African cities.

Inefficient Operations

Lack of smart routing and real-time tracking leads to delayed deliveries and poor customer experience.

Rider Income Instability & Exploitation

Most delivery riders in Nairobi operate without predictable income, insurance, or long-term security. High fuel costs, informal contracts, and platform commissions leave riders overworked, underpaid, and constantly at risk.

Our Solution

E-Fikishe combines electric vehicles, smart technology, and solar charging infrastructure to create a sustainable, scalable delivery platform.

Electric Fleet

Zero-emission electric bikes and tuk-tuks reduce operating costs by 70% and eliminate fuel expenses.

AI-Powered Dispatch

Smart routing algorithms optimize delivery routes, reduce delivery times, and maximize rider efficiency.

Solar Charging Network

Strategic solar charging stations ensure reliable power supply and create additional revenue streams.

Platform Features

Real-Time Tracking

Customers and businesses can track deliveries in real-time, improving transparency and customer satisfaction.

Dynamic Pricing

AI-driven pricing adjusts based on demand, distance, and time, ensuring competitive rates while maximizing revenue.

Rider Network

Flexible work opportunities for riders with training, support, and fair compensation structures.
